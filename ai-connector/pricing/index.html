Pricing questions

What counts as a data source?

One database or one folder you connect: for example the SQL Server database behind your accounting software. Shared folders and cloud drives can be connected from the Pro plan.

What counts as a question?

One message you send to the built-in AI assistant. A follow-up such as "only last month" is a new question.

What is a data lookup?

When you use your own Claude, Claude reads your data through lookups. One question usually takes 3 to 10 lookups, so 2,000 lookups is roughly 200 to 600 questions.

What happens when I reach my limit?

We warn you at 80% of your monthly limit. At 100% you can add 500 AI questions for €10 or move to a bigger plan. We never cut you off without giving you a choice.

Do I need a Claude subscription?

No. Every plan includes the built-in AI assistant. If your team already uses Claude, you can connect it too; your Claude plan is billed separately by Anthropic and must support custom connectors.

How does the free trial work?

You get 7 days on the plan you choose. A card is required to start. Cancel any time before day 8 and you pay nothing.

Are prices including VAT?

No. Prices are in euros, excluding VAT. VAT is added according to your country.

Can someone install it for us?

Yes. The Team plan includes a setup call with your IT. If you prefer we do everything, assisted setup starts at €490, one-off.

We are an IT provider or reseller. How does it work for us?

The Enterprise plan gives you volume pricing across your clients and onboarding for your technicians. Write to us.
